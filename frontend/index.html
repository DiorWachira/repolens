      § 02

      Method

      How the chart is read

      Each check is one line of the chart. A clear line scores 1, an advisory scores ½, a blocking line scores 0. The average becomes a health score out of 100, and that score is placed on a logMAR scale, so 100 reads the 20/20 line and 0 reads 20/200.

      The checks are deliberately small, portable and explainable. They are repository-level signals, not a code review, a dependency audit or a security certification.

      The examiner is optional. With Gemini selected, the report and your recent questions are sent to Google. Offline mode answers from the report alone.
